CAPTION:
Every goalkeeper coach knows the feeling. You've built something that works — a
warm-up sequence, a set-piece prep block, a final sharp-reactions set before the
keeper walks into the tunnel. It's dialled in. But then you're pulled away to a
tactical meeting, or your assistant runs the session alone, and somehow the
picture looks completely different.

That's not a people problem. It's a structure problem.

KEEPIX is built around repeatable session blocks — the kind you can hand to
another coach and trust that the keeper gets the same reps, the same reference
points, and the same physical cue to finish on. Nothing improvised. Nothing
dropped because someone forgot the order.

On match day especially, consistency matters more than creativity. Your keeper
doesn't need surprises ninety minutes before kick-off. They need a rhythm that
tells their body: we've done this, we know this, we're ready.

Set the routine once. Share it. Walk away knowing the work gets done the right way —
whether you're standing on the line or watching from the dugout.
