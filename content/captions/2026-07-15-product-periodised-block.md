HOOK (first line of caption): Most keeper plans live in three different PDFs and a notes app. Here's what one training block looks like when it's all in one place.

CAPTION:
Base phase. Build phase. Peak phase. Every goalkeeper coach knows the theory. The hard part is turning it into something you can actually run — session by session, week by week — without losing the thread halfway through pre-season.

KEEPIX lets you map a full periodised block from the ground up. Set your focus for each phase — movement foundations and set position in base, decision-making and crossing in build, match-intensity finishing work in peak — and keep every drill, progression, and note attached to the block itself. No separate files. No version confusion.

What changes on the pitch: your keeper moves through each phase with context. They can see where they've been, what they're working toward, and why today's session looks the way it does. That conversation alone is worth ten minutes of any warm-up.

For coaches managing more than one keeper, the structure holds across both. You're not rebuilding the block from scratch — you're adjusting load and emphasis, and the plan reflects that clearly.

Periodisation isn't a concept reserved for outfield players. Your keeper deserves the same structured thinking. KEEPIX is built to make that practical, not theoretical.
