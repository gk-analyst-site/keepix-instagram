CAPTION:
Between every rep, there's a window. Most coaches let it close.

The cue is "Reset."

Not "well done" — that ends the rep. Not "again" — that starts the next one too fast. Reset means: feet alive, hands up, eyes forward, weight where it needs to be. Two seconds. That's all it takes to build the habit that survives 87 minutes.

Three moments we use it:

1. After a save — keeper drops hands and relaxes. Say "reset" before the next ball is played. The shape is everything.

2. After a distribution error — before they start thinking about what went wrong. One word brings them back to the present rep.

3. Before a set piece — especially after a goal conceded. The keeper is in their head. "Reset" is a physical anchor. Feet, hands, eyes. Back to work.

The word only works if it means something. Build it in from day one. By the time the season matters, they do it without hearing it.
