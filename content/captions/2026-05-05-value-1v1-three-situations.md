HOOK (first line of caption): 1v1s aren't one problem. They're three — each with a different answer.

CAPTION:
Most keepers train the same 1v1 over and over: striker running through, GK comes out, get body in the way. That's one situation. It's not the whole picture.

Three situations we drill separately:

1. The breakaway — striker with space, GK closing. The decision is when to stop, not whether to come. Arrive too early and you're beaten around. Too late and the angle is gone. We drill the closing line, not the save.

2. The cutback — striker has cut inside, GK is set and waiting. The job is to stay big as long as possible. Do not dive early. Force the striker to commit first. We drill patience.

3. The smother — last resort, GK goes to ground. This gets scored on most often. Not because keepers dive wrong, but because they reach the smother three seconds too late. We drill the trigger, not the dive.

One coaching point per situation. Train them separately before you put them together.
