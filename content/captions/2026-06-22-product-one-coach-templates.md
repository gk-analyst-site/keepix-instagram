CAPTION:
Most goalkeeper coaches know this setup. You've got your keeper, your kit bag, and a wall of cones. No second pair of hands. No one to feed crosses while you're tracking footwork. Just you, making every minute count.

That's exactly the scenario we built KEEPIX session templates around.

Each template is structured for a single coach working solo — warm-up activation, technical block, competitive finishing. The sequencing is deliberate: exercises flow into each other without you having to reset the whole pitch between reps. Less time organising. More time coaching.

The templates cover the situations that actually come up in a real week: distribution under pressure, cross-claiming footwork, 1v1 angles, set-position mechanics. Not a generic warm-up library — a session you can run start to finish with confidence, even when the pitch is busy and the clock is tight.

Load it before you arrive. Run it. Adjust on the fly when your keeper needs more time on a specific pattern. The structure holds so you don't have to hold it all in your head.

Solo doesn't have to mean improvised.
