Bad footwork you can fix with reps. A keeper locked inside their own head is a different problem.

So: what's yours?

A physical reset — something small to do with their feet?
A question that brings them back to the present rep?
Just silence, and letting the next ball do the work?

There's no right answer here. We've tried all of them. What we've learned is that the right move is usually simpler than we expect.
