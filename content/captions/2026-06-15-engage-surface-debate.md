HOOK (first line of caption): Grass. Astro. Indoor. Coaches — which surface actually develops a keeper the most?

CAPTION:
Every surface teaches something different. Grass punishes a loose set position the moment the ball skids. Astro speeds everything up — footwork, reactions, decision-making — and there's nowhere to hide. Indoor strips the game back to pure technique and forces a keeper to move their feet properly when there's no give in the ground.

We've seen keepers transformed by a run of sessions on the wrong surface for their weakness. A keeper who's too static? Put them on astro. One who's sloppy with their hands? Indoor, no excuses.

But we're genuinely curious what coaches out there think. Surface availability shapes training for most of us — but if you could choose freely, where would you build a young keeper's foundation?

Drop your answer in the comments. Grass / Astro / Indoor — and tell us the one thing that surface teaches that the others can't.
