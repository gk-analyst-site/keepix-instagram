HOOK (first line of caption): Pre-season is the one window where you can actually set a baseline — don't waste it on guesswork.

CAPTION:
Most goalkeeper development gets measured in feelings. "He looked sharper today." "Her footwork's coming along." But feelings don't tell you whether your keeper improved by 10% or went backwards without you noticing.

A testing battery changes that. Four or five repeatable tests, run on day one of pre-season and again at week four, give you data you can stand behind — and conversations with keepers that go further than "keep working hard."

Here's a simple battery we've built around KEEPIX sessions:

• Set position reaction — keeper starts in base position, shot triggered at a fixed moment. Log response time and save percentage across 10 attempts.
• Lateral footwork — cones set at post width, keeper shuffles and recovers to centre. Time it. Do it tired, do it fresh.
• Distribution accuracy — driven throws and kicked distribution to marked targets at varying distances. Track hits out of 10.
• Cross claiming — height, timing, and contact quality rated across 8 deliveries. Simple 1–3 scale, noted immediately after each rep.

Run the same conditions every time: same surface, same time of day, same warm-up. KEEPIX lets you structure and repeat those reps consistently, so when the numbers move, you know it's the keeper that changed — not the session.

Progress you can measure is progress you can build on.
