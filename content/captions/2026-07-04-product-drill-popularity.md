HOOK (first line of caption): Your keepers vote with their feet. KEEPIX helps you hear them.

CAPTION:
You run a footwork circuit on Monday. Half the group is sharp, dialled in, competing for reps. On Thursday you run crossing work — same group, same energy on paper — but something's flat. They're going through the motions.

Most coaches chalk it up to a bad day. But what if it's information?

With KEEPIX, you can log which drills your keepers respond to — engagement, effort, the feel of a session — and look back across weeks of data. Patterns start to appear. A keeper who lights up in 1v1 work but switches off in distribution circuits might be telling you something about where their confidence lives. That's a coaching conversation waiting to happen.

It's not about letting your keepers pick the menu. It's about understanding what the data underneath a session is actually saying — so you can build progressions that challenge them where it's uncomfortable, and anchor sessions with the drills that pull the best work out of them.

Good session design isn't just about technical load. It's about attention, buy-in, and knowing your group. KEEPIX gives you a record to work from, so your gut has something to check itself against.
