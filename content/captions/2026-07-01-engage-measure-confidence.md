CAPTION:
Confidence in a goalkeeper is one of the strangest things to coach.

It doesn't show up in a clean stat. It's not always in the scoreline. But when it's there, you feel it on the pitch — the way they claim a cross without thinking twice, the way they set early on a one-v-one, the way the back four just... settles.

And when it's gone, you feel that too.

We've been thinking about what coaches actually look for — the real signals. Not "they made a good save." More like: they called for the ball again after a mistake. They held their set position even when the striker was closing fast. They distributed with intent instead of just clearing the danger.

So we want to hear from you.

What's the signal that tells you a keeper is back? The one moment in a session or a match where you think — yeah, they've got it again.

Drop it in the comments. Seriously — the best ones come from coaches who've been through it on a cold Tuesday night with a 17-year-old who just had a nightmare game.
