CAPTION:
One of the quietest problems in goalkeeper development isn't technical. It's logistical.

You build the week's GK sessions in your head, scribble the key points on a clipboard, brief your assistant five minutes before kick-off — and hope it lands. Sometimes it does. Sometimes the cues are different, the progressions get skipped, and two goalkeepers come away from the same week having trained completely different things.

With KEEPIX, you share the full session plan directly: the drill sequence, the key coaching points at each stage, the load for the day. Your assistant opens it on the touchline and runs exactly what you built. Same picture. Same language. Same standard.

It matters more than it sounds. Consistency in cues — "set early, weight forward" repeated the same way across every rep, every coach — is how technique actually beds in. One session won't change a goalkeeper. A hundred consistent ones will.

If you're working with a staff of two or more, KEEPIX gives you a single source of truth for every GK session on your calendar.
