CAPTION:
Every keeper has one. A line from a coach, a parent, a teammate — maybe even a stranger watching from the far touchline — that landed wrong and never quite left.

Maybe it was too vague to act on. Maybe it was brutally specific. Maybe it came at the worst possible moment, two minutes after a goal went in off your fingertips.

We've been thinking about the difference between feedback that builds and feedback that just… sits there. The stuff that sticks isn't always the most useful. Sometimes the words that stay with a keeper longest are the ones that needed more context, more timing, or more trust behind them.

So we want to hear from you — keepers and coaches alike.

What's the worst piece of feedback you ever received? And looking back, why do you think it stayed with you?

Drop it in the comments. No judgement — just a conversation worth having.
