CAPTION:
By the time the rep is over and you've processed what went wrong, the keeper has already moved on. The moment is gone.

Effective feedback is immediate, specific, and short. One thing. Five seconds.

Three formats we use:

1. Name + fix: "Feet — wider before the ball arrives." Not "your footwork was off." Tell them what to do, not what went wrong.

2. Question + pause: "Where were your hands?" Let them answer. They usually know. The question sticks longer than the correction.

3. Silence + next rep: Sometimes the best feedback is loading the next ball quickly. Dwell time is the enemy of confidence. Move on.

The mistake most coaches make is over-explaining. Three sentences in, the keeper has stopped listening. One sentence, then the next ball.
