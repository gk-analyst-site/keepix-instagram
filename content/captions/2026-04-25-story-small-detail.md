HOOK (first line of caption): A small detail most keeper coaches miss.

CAPTION:
Watch a keeper in the warm-up. Not the saves. The gap between reps.

That's where the habit lives.

The ones who reset into set position between every serve, hands already up, feet alive — they carry that shape into the match. The ones who stand up straight, drop their hands, and wait for the next cue — they'll do the same at 2-2 in the 87th minute. No amount of dive work fixes it if the reset is broken.

So during warm-ups, we stop coaching the save and start coaching the return. "Back to your feet, hands up, eyes on the ball." Every rep. Quietly, repeatedly.

Small detail. Decides a lot of goals.
