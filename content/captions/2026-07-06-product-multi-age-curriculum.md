CAPTION:
Most goalkeeper programmes fracture as they scale. You build something sharp for your senior keepers, then soften it for the younger groups until you're running five different sessions with five different philosophies. The keepers feel it. The coaches feel it.

KEEPIX is built around a single training framework that coaches adapt in intensity — not in principle. The footwork mechanics you're drilling with your U10s are the same ones underpinning your senior set position work. The language stays consistent. The progression is built in.

What that looks like in practice: a coach working across age groups runs one session plan, adjusts the load, and every keeper is moving through the same technical ladder — just at the rung that fits them. No dilution. No contradiction between what the younger groups hear on Tuesday and what the seniors run on Thursday.

The result isn't a tidy curriculum document. It's keepers who arrive at the next age group already speaking the language.
