CAPTION:
November. Wet pitch. The kind of session where nothing goes right.

A keeper — young, quiet, prone to overthinking — was having their worst day in weeks. Footwork wrong, distribution shaky, one cross claimed with eyes shut.

We came close to pulling them aside.

Instead, we said one thing: "Forget the last rep. What are your feet doing right now?"

They looked down. Adjusted. Two hops. Reset.

The next cross — clean claim, two-handed, loud call. The rep after, the same. By the end of the session they'd turned it around on their own. Not because of anything clever we said. Because we gave them something small to focus on instead of everything at once.

That's the job sometimes. Not tactics. Not drills. Just something small to come back to.
