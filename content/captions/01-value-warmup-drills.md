CAPTION:
A good warm-up isn't cardio. It's rehearsal. Before the first ball is struck, the keeper's feet, hands, and eyes need to already be in the session.

**1. Ladder → Set → Catch (4 mins)**
Quick feet through a 6-rung ladder, break into set position at the end, coach serves a chest-height ball. Reset, repeat both feet leading. Wakes up footwork and hand shape at the same time.

**2. Reaction Wall Tennis (3 mins)**
Keeper 2m from a wall, coach throws a tennis ball past them off the wall. One-handed catch, alternating sides. Short, sharp, builds eye tracking before you ever touch a full-size ball.

**3. Low-Dive Diagonals (4 mins)**
Two cones 4m apart. Keeper shuffles between them, coach rolls a ball to the far post as they arrive. Low dive, recover, back across. Grooves the collapse-dive shape without smashing hips on cold ground.

Keep it light. Keep it specific. Save the big work for the main block.
