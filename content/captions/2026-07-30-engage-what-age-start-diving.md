HOOK (first line of caption): At what age did you first teach a young keeper to dive — and how did you make it safe?

CAPTION:
It's one of those questions that splits the coaching room every time.

Some coaches won't go near diving technique until a keeper is 10 or 11 — letting them build footwork, angles, and confidence first. Others are on the ground from day one, introducing collapse dives as soon as a kid is ready to get their kit dirty.

There's no universal answer. Surface matters. Maturity matters. The individual keeper in front of you matters.

What we do know: the way you introduce it early shapes everything that follows — body shape, ground recovery, willingness to commit. Get the habit right and it sticks. Rush it and you spend years unpicking it.

So we're asking the coaches in this community: what's your age marker, and what's your first drill or progression when you decide a keeper is ready?

Drop it in the comments. Seriously — the variety of answers here is always one of the best conversations we have.
