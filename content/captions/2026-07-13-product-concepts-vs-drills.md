CAPTION:
Most coaches log drills. A link, a sketch, a PDF saved somewhere you'll never open again. The drill exists — but it doesn't connect to anything. There's no thread running from last Tuesday's crossing work to next week's distribution phase.

KEEPIX is built around logging concepts, not just drills.

A concept is the *why* behind the drill. "Narrow the angle on near-post saves." "Force the striker wide in 1v1." When you log at that level, every session you plan — every drill you pull — sits inside a bigger picture. You can look back at a goalkeeper's last six weeks and see what you've actually been building, not just what you've been doing.

The drill is the vehicle. The concept is the destination. Log the destination, and your drill library starts to work as a curriculum. Log only the drill, and you've got a well-organised folder of PDFs.

This is the shift KEEPIX was designed to support. Build sessions from concepts, track progress against them, and carry that curriculum with you pitch-side — not buried in a cloud folder.
