CAPTION:
Distribution is never just technique. The throw, the volley drop-kick, the rolled pass to the full-back — all of it lives or dies on the read that happens a second before.

Here's what we coach with KEEPIX keepers on building better distribution decisions:

**1. Scan before the save.**
The best distributors are reading the press *during* the shot or cross, not after. It sounds impossible until you train it. Start by calling out the shape of the press at the moment your keeper catches — not two seconds later. Make the verbal cue a habit in every repetition.

**2. Know your three exits before you have one.**
Coach your keeper to identify a near-side option, a switch option, and a safety valve (usually the CB drop) before the ball arrives. Decision speed is really option preparation. The keeper who scans early always looks calm.

**3. Match the tool to the pressure, not the preference.**
High press closing fast? The overarm throw is often quicker and more accurate to the near channel than a half-volley. Medium block with wingers tucked in? The switch kick opens the game. Drill each release against live, varied press shapes — not just against cones.

The read, the decision, the release. In that order, every time.
