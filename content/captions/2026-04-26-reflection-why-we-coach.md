CAPTION:
Not for the saves. Not for the clean sheets.

We coach for the keeper who was nervous in October and is organising their back four by March. For the one who used to freeze on crosses and now owns their six-yard box. For the quiet one who finally started shouting because they finally believed their voice mattered.

The scoreline is not the scoreboard.

Most of what we do will never show up on a highlight reel. It shows up in how a fourteen-year-old walks back to the goal after conceding, and whether their next save is braver than their last.

That's the job. That's enough.
