HOOK (first line of caption): Pre-season is the one window where technique gets your full attention — don't waste it.

CAPTION:
Once the season starts, sessions fill up with shape, set-pieces, and game prep. The goalkeeper's technical work gets squeezed into warm-ups and whatever minutes are left at the end.

Pre-season is different. You have time to be deliberate — to build the habits that hold up under pressure in October and November. That's exactly where KEEPIX earns its place in your programme.

Drop it into your first block of pre-season and use it to log what you're actually seeing: set position consistency, footwork patterns on mid-height shots, handling under fatigue in the final reps of a session. Not impressions — repeatable reference points.

When you can track how a keeper is moving across a week of sessions, you stop guessing. You make targeted decisions: more footwork volume here, less on the diving work there, a conversation about positioning on near-post crosses.

By the time your first competitive fixture arrives, your goalkeeper isn't just fit — they're technically grounded. That foundation is what lets the tactical layer land properly.

KEEPIX doesn't replace your eye for the game. It gives your eye somewhere to store what it sees.
