10 minutes. 3 drills. Sharper angles every time.

Angle play doesn't have to be a 45-minute session. These three footwork drills fit into your warm-up block and pay off the moment your keeper faces a 1v1.

—

🔵 DRILL 1 — POST-TO-POST SHUFFLE (3 mins)
Keeper starts on the near post. On your call, they shuffle to set position for a shot from the top of the box, recover, reset. Drive them through the footwork — small steps, weight forward, hands live. 4 sets, 45 seconds on.

—

🟡 DRILL 2 — CONE GATE APPROACH (4 mins)
Set two cones 2 yards apart at the penalty spot. Keeper starts on the line, moves out through the gate, sets. You serve a low driven shot — both sides. The gate forces them to find the right departure angle every rep. 6 reps each side.

—

🟢 DRILL 3 — THREE-BALL REACTION (3 mins)
Three balls on the six-yard arc. Keeper in set position. Point to a ball — they move, set, save the roll. Change the call before they finish setting. Chaos is the point. Fast feet, locked eyes.

—

Run this two sessions a week for a month. You'll see the difference in how quickly your keeper closes down space without overcommitting.
