CAPTION:
Building from the back sounds simple until the press arrives. The ball drops to your keeper, two forwards bearing down, and suddenly all that training on the training pitch feels very far away.

Here are three cues that help keepers stay composed and make clean decisions under pressure:

1 — "Angle early, not late."
Most keepers wait for the back pass to arrive before moving. Encourage them to read the situation one pass earlier — adjusting their body angle so the ball lands on the correct foot before any pressure is close. Shape first, receive second.

2 — "Scan before it hits your boot."
A quick check of the shoulder — ideally two — gives keepers the picture they need before they even touch the ball. Make it a habit in low-pressure rondos first, then build in a passive presser. The scan should feel automatic, not reactive.

3 — "Invite, then move."
Keepers who stand still while receiving invite pressure. Teach them to take a positive touch away from the press — even a yard — to create an angle for the next player. It shifts the dynamic from 'under pressure' to 'in control'.

Run these cues in a simple 3v2 build-out shape this week and watch how quickly your keeper's decision-making changes. The cue should come from you once; after that, your keeper should hear it in their own head.
