HOOK (first line of caption): The 30-second warm-up cue we never skip.

CAPTION:
Before anything else — before handling, before the first ball — 30 seconds of this:

Feet shoulder-width. Small, fast hops. Every three hops, drop into set position and hold for one second. Reset, hops again. Repeat for 30 seconds.

That's it.

It wakes the calves, the quads, and the set shape at the same time. By the time the first serve comes, the keeper is already standing the way they want to save.

Use it as a ritual, not a drill. Same spot, same rhythm, every session. The keeper stops thinking about it, which is the whole point.
