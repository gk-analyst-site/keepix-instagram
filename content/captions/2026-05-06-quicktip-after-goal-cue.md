CAPTION:
The ball hits the net. The keeper's head drops. You have about ten seconds before the restart — and most coaches don't use them.

Here's what we say:

"Head up. Feet. Next ball."

Three words. In that order. Every time.

Not "it's okay" — that's noise. Not "we'll fix it after" — that delays the focus they need right now. Not "stay in the game" — too abstract for a keeper in the moment.

Head up — posture first. Everything follows posture.
Feet — back to set position. Alive, not planted.
Next ball — the only thing that matters now.

Two seconds to say. Needs a full season of repetition before they hear it automatically.

Start using it now so it means something when it counts.
