You know the feeling. You've built the week around a specific footwork block — collapse-step angles, positioning on near-post deliveries, a sharp 1v1 sequence to finish. It's written out, timed, purposeful.

Then the session starts. One goalkeeper arrives late. A different one needs more reps on something you thought was already dialled in. You adapt on the fly, and somewhere in the middle of it all, you lose the thread of what actually got done.

That gap — between the plan and the reps — is where KEEPIX fits.

With KEEPIX, you're not guessing at volume or second-guessing your progressions. You can see exactly which reps were completed, where in the session the work happened, and what still needs addressing before the next block. The plan stays connected to the pitch.

Coaches who use KEEPIX don't train less creatively. They just stop losing the detail. What you designed, you can track. What you tracked, you can build on.

That's what closing the gap looks like.
