CAPTION:
Match week is when preparation stops being general and starts being specific. If you already know the opposition likes to whip early crosses from deep, your goalkeeper shouldn't be spending Tuesday doing flat, predictable shot-stopping reps.

With KEEPIX, you can reshape a session around exactly what's coming on Saturday. Pull up the patterns you've charted — wide deliveries, second-post runs, early-ball cut-backs — and build your drills around those moments. Your keeper gets hundreds of reps against the exact type of service they'll face, not a random selection of whatever felt good to throw at them.

We ran this with a group last week. The coach identified two recurring threats: diagonal through-balls in behind and low-driven near-post crosses. Every KEEPIX rep that session was locked into one of those two scenarios. By Thursday, the decision-making was sharper, the starting position more deliberate. The keeper wasn't surprised by anything on game day.

The details matter at this level. Footwork from an adjusted angle. The half-second earlier set position on a cross. Those small adjustments only stick with volume — and volume only comes when the prep is focused.

Match week doesn't have to be a guessing game. It can be a plan.
